GOOD TO KNOW

Questions?
We've got answers.

Everything you need to know before bringing CoinWorldCap into your Telegram chats.

Explore the bot on Telegram
Is the CoinWorldCap Telegram Bot free?

Yes. The bot is designed to be free to use. All you need is a Telegram account.

How do I get market signals in a private chat?

Open the bot and send /start. This subscribes you to signals in your private chat. Use /signals to choose signal types, /watch to add assets, or /stop to unsubscribe.

I added the bot to my group. Why aren't alerts arriving?

Push signals are off by default in other groups. A group admin must send /startpush to turn them on. The official CoinWorldCap community deliberately gets Top News and command replies instead of automatic signal pushes.

Why isn't the bot answering commands in my group?

If other bots are active, send a directed command such as /btc@CWC_Test_Bot. For reliable plain commands like /btc, the group owner can make the bot an admin with the minimum permissions the Telegram client permits.

How can I reduce or pause notifications?

Use /signals to switch individual alerts on or off, or /mute 30m, /mute 2h, or /mute 8h to pause all pushes temporarily. /mute off resumes them. /stopsignals disables signals but keeps digests enabled; /stop unsubscribes from private-chat pushes.

Can the bot read messages in my Telegram group?

Telegram's bot privacy mode stays enabled. When the bot is an admin, Telegram can deliver regular group messages to it, but the bot is designed to discard non-command messages immediately and not store ordinary chat content.

Can I track specific coins or DEX tokens?

Yes. Add up to 20 assets with /watch and see them with /watchlist. Custom watch rules support different thresholds and time windows; DEX-token checks depend on the token appearing on a tracked DexScan list.

Does the bot support languages other than English?

The first phase supports English only. More languages, starting with Chinese, are planned for a later phase.

Are the signals trading recommendations?

No. Signals are data-based market notifications for information only, not financial advice. Crypto markets are volatile and you should make your own decisions.
